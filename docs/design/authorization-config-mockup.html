Taste-calls for Ryan

  • The read-out reads as a configuration: clicking a chip is a neutral config control — Allow / Restricted + lock, with a quiet "set at the agent level" scope-note. No "just this agent," no "narrowest wins," no full cascade — it configures a setting, it doesn't explain a verdict. Neutral enough now?
  • Permissive-default, named: the header states agents are trusted (on-by-default, restrict-the-exception) — a deliberate divergence from the least-privilege norm. Does framing it as intentional read right?
  • Context-driven groups: all-default groups are a quiet one-line summary ("Messaging — all on"); a group with a restriction/assignment auto-opens + its summary stands out (red "1 restricted" / blue "dev drives 2"). So you scan exceptions, not 30 rows. Right behaviour?
  • Group summaries: the collapsed line reads the resolved state at a glance ("Iteration ops — pm drives · 6"). Is the summary enough, or do you want a peek of which functions on hover?
  • Grouping shape: Capabilities → Messaging / Presence / Events; Workflow → Lifecycle / Review gates / Iteration ops / Epic ops. Are these the right buckets, or grouped some other way?
  • Scale check: ~6 capabilities + ~15 workflow-steps collapse to ~3 + ~4 group-lines until you open one. Does that feel reason-about-able at full size?
