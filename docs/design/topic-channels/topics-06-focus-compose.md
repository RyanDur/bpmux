## Visual notes

- Focus mode (#181) composes with topics: filter by role WITHIN a topic
- Dev's messages in "teaching protocol" are hidden (dev isn't in focus)
- Title bar shows "[focus: pm,designer]" so the user knows the filter is active
- Two orthogonal filters compose cleanly:
  - Topic = WHAT we're talking about
  - Focus = WHO I'm listening to
