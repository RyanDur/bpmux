Reference — what "other comment modal boxes" do (your ask)

GitHub's "Add a comment"/close-with-comment, Linear's comment composer, VA/Carbon dialog: all share the same proximity rhythm — a clear header set apart, a label hugging its field, and the primary action in a distinct bottom-right zone with generous separation. None of them space the header, field, and footer equally. The proposed modal adopts that rhythm on our own (current) palette.
