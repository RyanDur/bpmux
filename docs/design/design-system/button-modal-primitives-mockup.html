Darcula design-system — button & modal primitives

Slice 2 of the design-system, on the Darcula foundation. Ryan's button/modal research grammar (the structural rules) laid onto the Darcula palette (the visual base). These are the two most-reused primitives — get them right and every dialog/action across board, sidebar, chat inherits the same feel. Mockup-first; rendered at the real sizes so you can judge the feel. Not shipped tokens — taste-review first.

1 · Buttons

Size scale — fixed heights (the new grammar)

Today a button's height is an accident of padding: 6px 16px + line-height (.drill-in-btn) — no height token, so "a button" is a different physical size in every panel and the 44px touch-target isn't guaranteed. The grammar makes height a named decision: Small 32 · Medium 40 (default) · Large 48, horizontal padding in a 2:1 rhythm (12/16/20), and a 44px tap-area baked in even on the 32px small (invisible inset).

2 · Modals

Size scale — responsive %-widths (not fixed px)

Today the reject-modal is a fixed max-width:32rem. The grammar scales to the window: XS 24–48% (confirmations, the typed-note reject) · M 48–60% (default forms) · L 60–80% (rich content). Centered, dimmed backdrop, elevated. Shown proportionally inside a mock app-window:

What this gets you: every dialog and action in the app inherits ONE button-size scale + ONE modal-anatomy. The reject-modal, edit-story, timeline, confirmations all read as the same family — you learn the rhythm once. The 44px tap-target + 3px focus-ring + native-<dialog> semantics come baked into the primitive (accessibility by construction, not a later pass).

Taste-calls for you:
  • Primary fill — filled-blue #4e7ec2 for the default action (IntelliJ's default-button look), or a more restrained grey-with-blue-border?
  • Danger — red-outline ghost (shown, Darcula-restrained), or a filled-red for destructive weight?
  • Radius — 6px buttons / 8px modals (softer), or tighter 4px to match today?
  • Default modal size — is M (48–60%) the right default, with XS for the reject/confirm?
