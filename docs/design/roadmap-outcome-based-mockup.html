Outcome-based roadmap — outcomes by sequence (the why), drill → the work

The roadmap's only unit is the OUTCOME (a measurable behavior-change), sequenced Now / Next / Later (commitment-order, not calendar), each with its why (benefit · success-criteria · confidence) + readiness-to-convene. Drill an outcome → the iterations that build up to produce it (in order) → convene-checks. Epics are a board artifact (the work-grouping for stories) — not surfaced in the roadmap; outcomes are the roadmap unit (the why), produced by a sequence of iterations (the build-up).
